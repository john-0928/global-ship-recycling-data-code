# Third-party data notice

## NGO Shipbreaking Platform

The study's processed dismantling dataset is derived from the annual lists of ships dismantled worldwide published by the NGO Shipbreaking Platform for 2014–2025. It is not included in this code-only package. The source must be credited in the manuscript and metadata. No vessel-level records should be added without confirmed redistribution permission.

Source landing page: https://shipbreakingplatform.org/annual-lists/

## Clarksons Research

The active-fleet register and market series obtained from the Shipping Intelligence Network of Clarksons Research are third-party commercial data and are not included. The licence restriction applies to vessel-level records and underlying proprietary series. Eligible researchers must request access directly from Clarksons Research under its standard terms.

This repository contains authors’ analysis code and documentation only. It must not contain the licensed vessel-level register, extracted Clarksons market series, input templates populated from restricted sources or derived result tables.

## ITF flag list and EU institutional data

The repository records the classifications used by the authors. Users should consult the cited official sources for authoritative and updated classifications. The classifications in this package are frozen to the study version and should not be silently updated after publication.
